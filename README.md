## 🕹️ About

Car Mechanic Simulator 2026 is a vehicle repair and workshop-management simulation built around diagnosing, rebuilding, customizing, and trading cars. This unofficial Windows build surfaced ahead of schedule in a raw, unfinished state, preserving the garage grind, co-op workshop play, vehicle collection, racing locations, and deep part-based repairs.

For gearheads and simulation fans who enjoy methodical repairs, garage progression, car collecting, and an unpolished early-build atmosphere.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🔧 **Deep Vehicle Repairs** — Diagnose, remove, repair, and install thousands of individual car parts.
- 🚗 **150+ Cars** — Restore vehicles across more than 500 configurations and rarity levels.
- 🤝 **Co-op Workshop** — Build and manage your garage together with friends.
- 🏚️ **Junkyards and Barns** — Find abandoned vehicles, recover parts, and uncover procedurally generated content.
- 🏁 **Racing Locations** — Take rebuilt cars to the race track, rally track, or airport.
- 🛠️ **Garage Customization** — Decorate and shape your workshop with tools, furniture, lighting, and more.
- 🌙 **Living Workshop** — Manage deliveries, fuel, time, jobs, and a full day-and-night cycle.
- 🎨 **Custom Liveries** — Create custom vehicle designs using liveries and stickers.
